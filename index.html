California Apartment Association PAC (CAAPAC)

The political action committee of California's largest landlord and property manager trade group, funded by its rental-industry members.

National Association of Realtors Fund

The campaign spending arm of the National Association of Realtors, which outspent every other organization in the country on federal lobbying in 2024*. Locally, it can spend unlimited sums on ads for candidates it favors.

California Real Estate PAC (CREPAC)

The PAC of the California Association of Realtors, which poured at least $19 million into defeating Prop 33†, the statewide 2024 ballot measure that would have let cities expand rent control.

Harbor Village Mobile Home Park

Residents of this 300-lot park own their homes but rent the land beneath them, leaving them few options when lot rents rise. Its owner, Marjal Corp., is part of a group of statewide property management firms operated by Albert Engel.

* OpenSecrets federal lobbying data, 2024, as reported by Real Estate News, "NAR spent more on lobbying than any other group in 2024," Feb. 14, 2025. † Jeremia Kimelman, "This California proposition is drawing the most campaign spending," CalMatters, Oct. 29, 2024.

The same landlord money that backed our council is now bankrolling the fight against renter protections.

Between 2018 and 2024, the three biggest spenders in council races put $81,100 into getting their candidates elected. In just over a month this fall, those same three interests have poured $150,000 into defeating Measure E, the ballot measure to strengthen renter protections in Redwood City.
